## Properties pinned by tests (not just asserted in prose)
1. Fixed suffix ALWAYS appended — even when nothing spoken/withheld
   (test: suffix present on empty assembly AND on full assembly).
2. No counts / ordinals / depths / sensitivities / item-ids in ANY field;
   wrapper introduces no digits (fail-fast _assert_wrapper_clean on every
   assembly, not just in tests).
3. Probe vocabulary is ONE argless constant sentence — identical across
   audiences and across worlds (host never forwards probes to a data path).
4. Chunked turns: "next" re-plans remaining items against FRESH belief
   (audience change between turns collapses at the turn boundary);
   replay is idempotent (no advance, no double exposure commit);
   withdraw drops chunk state; digest ALWAYS queued silently.
5. Exposure budgets: pending exposures committed AFTER speaking, only for
   items ACTUALLY spoken (S2's commit-after-speaking, chunk-aware).
6. keep_secret TIGHTEN edges apply via the min-only overlay BEFORE planning
   (clearance drops immediately on the next brief).
7. every one of the 6 tools returns the sealed-speech contract;
   content[0].text IS speak verbatim (envelope property asserted per call).
8. day-card resource returns the contract (F1 count-leak closed on the
   resource path too).
